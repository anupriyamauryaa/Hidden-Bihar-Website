1. Mahabodhi Temple

About the Place

The Mahabodhi Temple in Bodh Gaya is a UNESCO World Heritage Site where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment. It is one of the holiest Buddhist pilgrimage sites in the world.

2. Bodhi tree,Bodh gaya

About the Place

Nalanda Mahavihara was one of the world's oldest universities. Students from many countries came here to study Buddhism, science and philosophy.

3. Great Budha Statue,Bodh gaya

About the Place

Takht Sri Patna Sahib is the birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh Ji and is one of the five Takhts of Sikhism.

4. Durgeshwari Caves,Bodh gaya

About the Place

Located in Rajgir, the Vishwa Shanti Stupa is a symbol of peace and offers beautiful panoramic views of the surrounding hills.

5. Nalanda Mahavihara

About the Place

Valmiki Tiger Reserve is Bihar's only tiger reserve and is famous for its forests, wildlife and rich biodiversity.

6. xuanzang Memorial Hall,Nalanda

About the Place

Vikramshila was an ancient Buddhist university established by King Dharmapala during the Pala dynasty.

7. Rajgir

About the Place

Golghar is a historic granary in Patna built by the British in 1786. It provides an excellent view of the city.

8. Vulture Peak ,Rajgir

About the Place

Bihar Museum showcases the rich history, culture, art and heritage of Bihar through modern galleries.

9. Venu Vanar

About the Place

Pawapuri Jal Mandir is a famous Jain pilgrimage site where Lord Mahavira attained Nirvana.

10. Vaisali

About the Place

Griddhakuta Hill in Rajgir is where Lord Buddha delivered many important sermons to his followers.

11. Ashoka Pillar,Vaishali

About the Place

Patna Museum houses ancient sculptures, coins, paintings and historical artifacts from Bihar.

12.Budha Relic Stupa,Vaisali

About the Place

Barabar Caves are the oldest surviving rock-cut caves in India and date back to the Mauryan period.

13. Budha Relic Stupa,Vaisali

About the Place

Kesaria Stupa is one of the tallest and largest Buddhist stupas in the world.

14. Vikramshila University,Bhagalpur

About the Place

Mundeshwari Temple is considered one of the oldest functional Hindu temples in India.

15. Buddha Smriti Park,Patna

About the Place

Sita Kund is a famous hot water spring associated with the Ramayana and is visited by pilgrims throughout the year.
